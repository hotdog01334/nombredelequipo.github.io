What type of content did we choose?

We chose educational content that teaches people about mental health since we feel like its a topic people need to learn more about nowadays, especially during a time where theres a new crisis every week

Which platform are we streaming on?

We chose YouTube since it is already a well-known site and also because one of the most popular streamers (Ishowspeed) started there

Who is our target audience?

We consider our content to be suitable for most audiences. However, we consider our content to be best for adolescents that are struggling with their mental health, since they might not know what do yet and/or how to open up to someone about their problems

What does our audience need?

Our audience has a plethora of needs we must try to abide to as much as possible
these include:
  • Understanding Boundaries
  • Informative Content
  • Respectful Conversations
  • Relevant Topics
  • Engaging Content

What will our actual content look like?

Our content will look pretty similar to those videos where people interview others on the street. But ours will specifically be about their mental health. And we'll also make content that can help educate people about their problems using these real experiences as references
